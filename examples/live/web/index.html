The DOM over a wire

The board on the left is a component whose program does not touch this page: it runs in RemoteDOM, and what it does to its DOM goes, as JSON, to a client in the page that does it, while the events the page hears come back to the program. Here both ends are in this page, and what crosses between them is shown on the right. The same component served by halogen-example-live-server runs natively, and every tab that opens it shows one board.
